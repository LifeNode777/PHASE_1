## Interpretation
### Observations
- Both restored repositories show short, clone-dominated pulses within 09/24–09/27 with high distinct-cloner counts (96 and 35 unique cloners in 14 days).
- The XPT pulse is a second wave: the first was temporally adjacent to the 21.09 sim/ artifact publication; the second is larger and offset by ~4 days.
- The META_Codex pulse coincides with renewed author-side activity on that repository (contributions 1 → 3) and with multi-visitor views of /discussions and /issues.
- Referrer structure remains thin (one internal referrer row per repository); clone events carry no referrer information, so the absent btglss.net trace constrains only web-view referrals.
### Interpretations
- Pulse shape (sharp, clone-dominated, many unique cloners, simultaneous in two functionally unrelated repositories) is consistent with an automated or bulk acquisition sweep (mirror sync, crawler/aggregator sweep, bulk downloading) touching the ecosystem, rather than organic readership growth.
- The XPT second wave exceeding the publication-day peak is consistent with delayed propagation of the 21.09 sim/ artifacts (indexing, listing or referral maturation) or with a new exposure event around 09/24–25; the data cannot separate these.
- META_Codex's shift from marginal to pulsed, together with /discussions and /issues multi-visitor traces, is consistent with discovery by an audience interacting through GitHub surfaces rather than passive reading.
- The contribution rebalancing (TOKIO share loosening; Xeno and META rising) describes author-side activity redistribution; it overlaps the traffic pulses in time but is evidentially independent of them.
### Hypotheses
- A common external driver (aggregator listing, bot network, mirror fleet, dataset crawl) swept multiple LifeNode repositories around 09/24–27. Falsifiable prediction: when the remaining panels restore, TOKIO/PHASE_1/LifeNode_2.0 daily charts should show clone pulses on the same dates if this holds.
- Alternatively, the XPT second wave is delayed technical diffusion of the sim/ contract among many distinct human actors (96 unique cloners), with the META_Codex pulse as spillover exploration via the profile.
- The outage onset coinciding with the TOKIO spike (23.09) is most parsimoniously a platform-side coincidence; no supported causal link.
